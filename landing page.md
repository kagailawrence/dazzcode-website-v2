What should NOT be on the landing page

I would actively remove or reduce:

Too much abstract jargon

Words like:

institutional-grade
protocol
execution
engineering assets
alpha
transmission

A few can make the brand distinctive. Too many make the company harder to understand.

Huge lists of services

Don't put:

Mobile Apps + SEO + UI/UX + APIs + AI + databases + CTO + DevOps + blockchain + ...

That weakens your positioning.

Unverified statistics

Don't use a number just because it looks good.

Huge blocks of text

Visitors should be able to scan the page.

Excessive animation

The product itself should be the visual hero.

What makes the design look "pro"

A professional Dazzcode landing page isn't about having the most effects.

I would aim for:

Typography

Large, confident headings.

Spacing

Lots of breathing room.

Grid

Very clean alignment.

Color

Dark premium base + one strong accent.

Images

Real product screenshots.

Motion

Subtle and purposeful.

Cards

Minimal borders and restrained shadows.

Copy

Simple, direct, confident.

Proof

Real projects, real outcomes, real people.

The biggest design principle for Dazzcode

Your current style can remain premium and technical, but the visual hierarchy should become:

MESSAGE
   ↓
PRODUCT
   ↓
PROOF
   ↓
PROCESS
   ↓
TECHNOLOGY

Not:

ANIMATION
   ↓
ABSTRACT GRAPHICS
   ↓
BIG CLAIM
   ↓
MORE ANIMATION
   ↓
TECHNICAL JARGON

That change alone can make the site feel substantially more credible.
