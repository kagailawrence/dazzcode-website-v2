## 2. What Affects the Cost of a Website in Kenya?

A website quote is not an arbitrary number. Six core factors determine the final price:

### A. Number of Pages & Content Structure
A simple 5-page business website (Home, About, Services, Contact, Blog) takes less design and copywriting work than a 30-page corporate portal with dedicated service pages and case studies.

### B. Custom Design vs. Pre-made Templates
A generic WordPress template bought online can be put together quickly, but it often comes with heavy code, security vulnerabilities, and slow load times. Bespoke, handcrafted web development ensures your brand stands out and loads under 1 second on Kenyan mobile networks (Safaricom, Airtel).

### C. Functional Complexity & Custom Features
Interactive features such as WhatsApp instant routing, booking forms, downloadable PDF resources, dynamic calculators, and custom quote request flows require dedicated development and testing.

### D. Payment & API Integrations
Integrating automated **Lipa na M-Pesa STK Push** callbacks, CRM synchronizations, SMS notifications, or Google Maps APIs requires backend development to ensure security and reliability.

### E. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) & Copywriting
A website is useless if prospective Kenyan clients cannot find it on Google. On-page SEO, schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimization, and keyword-targeted copywriting add immense business value to your initial build.

### F. Hosting & Infrastructure
Running your site on reliable Linux VPS servers, Docker containers, or edge CDNs with free automated SSL certificates ensures 99.9% uptime compared to cheap shared hosting servers that crash frequently.

## 3. Website vs. Web Application: Which One Do You Need?

Understanding the difference saves you money:

- **A Website** is primarily informational. It explains who you are, what you offer, showcases testimonials, and encourages prospects to call, email, or chat on WhatsApp.
- **A Web Application** is interactive software. Users log in, submit data, generate invoices, track deliveries, view private dashboards, or manage internal company operations.

## 4. Why Cheap Websites Often Cost More in the Long Run

Many Kenyan business owners fall into the trap of hiring cheap freelancers charging KSh 5,000 to KSh 10,000. Common pitfalls include:

1. **Abandoned Projects:** Freelancers disappearing midway through development.
2. **Hidden Annual Fees:** Shock charges for domain renewals or basic plugin updates.
3. **Slow Mobile Performance:** Websites taking 8+ seconds to load, causing 60%+ of mobile visitors to bounce.
4. **No Security:** Unpatched WordPress plugins resulting in hacked databases and spam redirects.
