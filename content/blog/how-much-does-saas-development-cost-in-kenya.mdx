## 2. Why Choose a Specialized Engineering Partner Over Cheap Freelancers?

Building SaaS is fundamentally different from designing a standard WordPress website:
1. **Financial Security**: M-Pesa webhooks must be protected with signature verification and transaction idempotency to prevent fraud.
2. **Offline Resilience**: As detailed in our [DazzPOS Kenya Case Study](/case-studies/dazzpos), intermittent internet requires local caching architectures.
3. **Institutional Code**: Clean TypeScript and PostgreSQL schemas ensure your startup can easily raise seed capital without facing total code rewrites.
