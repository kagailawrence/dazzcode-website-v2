To hire a reliable SaaS development agency, you must evaluate them based on their commitment to institutional-grade engineering and complete IP transfer, rather than just hourly rates. Look for a technical partner that operates transparently through dedicated communication channels (like Slack), utilizes modern stacks (such as Next.js and TypeScript), guarantees the delivery of fully documented source code with zero vendor lock-in, and focuses on rapid "Lean Production" rather than bloated, endless development cycles.

## Red Flags to Avoid

Many non-technical founders burn their initial runway on the wrong partners. Watch out for these warning signs:
1. **Opaque Code Ownership:** If the agency retains rights to your core IP or tries to lock you into their proprietary CMS, walk away.
2. **Outdated Stacks:** Agencies pushing legacy frameworks (like outdated PHP setups for demanding real-time apps) are optimizing for their comfort, not your scalability.
3. **"Yes Men":** A good agency acts as a fractional CTO. If they blindly agree to build every feature you suggest without validating its impact on your MVP roadmap, they are misaligning incentives to rack up billable hours.
