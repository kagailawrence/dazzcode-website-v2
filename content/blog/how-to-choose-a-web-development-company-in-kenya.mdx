With hundreds of agencies and freelance developers offering web design services across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and Nakuru, choosing the right partner for your business website or custom web application can feel overwhelming.

Many business owners have experienced the frustration of hiring a developer who delivers a slow, template-cluttered website, stops responding to phone calls right after payment, or cannot integrate local Kenyan payments like M-Pesa.

To protect your investment, here is a practical guide on how to choose a reliable **web development company in Kenya**.

---

## 1. Look for Real Technical Depth (Not Just "Template Designers")

Many web design agencies in Kenya only know how to install off-the-shelf WordPress themes and visual page builders (like Elementor or Divi). While fine for basic brochures, page builders often produce slow, bloated code that ranks poorly on Google and breaks whenever WordPress updates.

Ask prospective partners:
- **Do you write clean, custom code or just modify templates?**
- **What modern frameworks do you work with?** (e.g., Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Go, PostgreSQL).
- **Can you build custom backend logic and database workflows if our business needs grow?**

A true web development company can build both lightweight marketing websites and complex web applications.

---

## 2. Check Kenyan Payment & Integration Expertise

If you do business in Kenya, payment integration is critical. Your developer must understand:
- **Safaricom Daraja API (Lipa na M-Pesa STK Push):** Automated popups on customer phones and instant callback validation.
- **WhatsApp API & Lead Routing:** Direct click-to-chat triggers with automated pre-filled inquiry text.
- **SMS Gateways:** SMS notifications via Africa's Talking or Advanta Africa.

Ask to see examples of live systems they have integrated with Kenyan payment providers.

---

## 3. Verify Speed & Mobile Optimization

Over 75% of internet traffic in Kenya occurs on smartphones over mobile cellular data networks (Safaricom 4G/5G, Airtel). If a website takes longer than 3 seconds to load, potential clients will hit the back button.

Test their portfolio websites on [Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev):
- Do their websites score 90+ on mobile performance?
- Are images properly compressed in next-gen formats (WebP/AVIF)?
- Are fonts and scripts loaded efficiently without blocking page rendering?

---

## 5. Ask About Post-Launch Support & SEO

A website is not finished the day it goes live. You need to ensure your agency provides:
1. **Ongoing Security & Maintenance:** Regular software updates, vulnerability patching, and database backups.
2. **Technical SEO:** Proper heading tags (H1, H2), Schema.org structured data, meta tags, and Google Search Console sitemap indexing.
3. **Dedicated Hosting / VPS Deployment:** Reliable server setup with SSL certificates and fast uptime.

## 6. Summary Checklist Before Signing a Contract

Before paying a deposit, make sure your web developer guarantees:
- [x] Full ownership of domain, source code, and design assets
- [x] Responsive, mobile-first design tested on real devices
- [x] Fast page load speeds (< 1.5 seconds)
- [x] Direct Kenyan payment integrations (M-Pesa, card)
- [x] Basic on-page SEO setup and Google indexing
- [x] Clear SLA for maintenance and bug fixes
