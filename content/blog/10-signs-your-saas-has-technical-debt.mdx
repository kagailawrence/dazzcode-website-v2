Technical debt is the silent killer of scaling software products. In the early days, cutting corners to launch an MVP is understandable. However, as user numbers grow, unaddressed technical debt compounds until simple feature updates take weeks and database crashes become frequent.

Here are the **10 critical warning signs** that your SaaS codebase requires immediate remediation:

---

1. **Developers Are Terrified to Deploy on Fridays**: The lack of automated tests means every release risks breaking production.
2. **Simple Features Take Weeks to Ship**: Changing a single form input requires modifying 6 different disconnected files.
3. **504 Gateway Timeouts During Peak Hours**: The database runs out of connections because pooling is missing.
4. **Massive Cloud Hosting Bills**: AWS or Vercel compute bills double every month without proportional user growth.
5. **Slow API Response Times (> 800ms)**: Sluggish page loads that cause user frustration and trial churn.
6. **Copy-Pasted Business Logic**: The same calculations or validation rules exist in multiple controllers.
7. **Deprecated Dependencies with Known CVEs**: NPM packages that haven't been updated in 3+ years.
8. **Lack of Tenant Isolation**: High risk of data leaks between competing organizations on your platform.
9. **No Error Tracking or Observability**: You only find out about bugs when angry customers email support.
10. **Onboarding a New Developer Takes 3 Weeks**: Fragile local development setups that fail without manual patching.
