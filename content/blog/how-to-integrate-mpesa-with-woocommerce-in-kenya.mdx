In Kenya, over 80% of online shopping transactions happen through **Safaricom M-Pesa**. For WooCommerce store owners, providing a smooth, automated M-Pesa payment experience is the single highest-leverage optimization to reduce cart abandonment.

In this guide, we explore how M-Pesa integration works on WooCommerce, the difference between manual Paybill entry and **Daraja STK Push**, and how automated webhooks keep your accounting clean.

---

## 1. Manual Paybill vs. Lipa na M-Pesa STK Push

There are two primary ways Kenyan stores accept M-Pesa:

### The Legacy Manual Method (High Cart Abandonment)
1. Customer reaches checkout and is given a Paybill number and Account number.
2. Customer leaves their mobile browser, opens the M-Pesa SIM Toolkit, and manually enters the details.
3. Customer receives a Safaricom SMS receipt, copies the 10-character transaction code (e.g. `QKT892H10`), and pastes it back into the website.
4. Store owner has to manually verify if money arrived before shipping the product.

### The Modern STK Push Method (Automated in 0.8s)
1. Customer enters their Safaricom mobile number at checkout.
2. An instant PIN prompt pops up on their handset screen automatically.
3. The customer enters their 4-digit M-Pesa PIN.
4. Safaricom's Daraja API fires an instant encrypted webhook callback to your WooCommerce server.
5. The order status automatically switches to **"Processing"**, inventory is decremented, and an SMS receipt is dispatched.

## 2. Technical Architecture of Daraja 2.0 Webhooks

To ensure zero payment leakage and complete security, a production WooCommerce M-Pesa integration requires:
- **SSL/TLS 1.3 Encryption**: All API communication with Safaricom endpoints must be securely encrypted.
- **Idempotent Transaction Handling**: Ensuring a single M-Pesa transaction receipt cannot be submitted twice to prevent duplicate order fraud.
