## 2. What Factors Drive WooCommerce Development Costs?

The final investment for your online store depends on several technical and operational factors:

### A. Payment Gateway & M-Pesa Integration
Kenyan consumers expect instant **Lipa na M-Pesa STK Push** prompts at checkout. Setting up automated callbacks with Safaricom's Daraja API 2.0 ensures orders update immediately to "Processing" without manual transaction code verification.

### B. Catalog Complexity & Variations
A store selling 10 simple products takes less engineering time than a fashion boutique with 200 products, each having multiple size, color, and stock variations.

### C. Custom Shipping Logic
Do you need flat-rate delivery for Nairobi, motorcycle courier rates for Westlands and Kilimani, or parcel couriers for upcountry towns like Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, and Eldoret? Custom delivery matrices require specialized calculation rules.

### D. Hosting Infrastructure (VPS vs Shared Hosting)
Budget shared hosting often crashes during high-traffic promotional campaigns. Deploying your WooCommerce store on a dedicated Linux VPS with Redis object caching ensures sub-second page loads. Learn more about our [Linux VPS Deployment Services](/services/vps-deployment).
